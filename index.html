Lucid AI Creations

We partner with organizations supporting at-risk and vulnerable populations, and help them build AI tools that get those people the support they need faster and with less friction.

Imagine an AI tool that could —

Turn a survivor's evidence into a court-ready file Walk someone step by step through a disability claim Help a family with a sick child prepare everything they need for an insurance claim Guide an older person through applying for the aged care services they're entitled to Guide someone through what to expect in an asylum interview, step by step

Once it's built, we hand the tool over to that organization and train the staff — so they can train everyone who comes through their door needing help. Then we step back. For free. Always.

Why this matters

This is what's happening right now, without tools like these.

A survivor spends money she doesn't have paying a lawyer to sort and organize documents she could have done herself.

Someone represents themselves in court with no idea what to expect, and loses ground they might have held with basic preparation.

A caseworker spends an entire afternoon helping one person fill out a form, while ten others wait for help that won't come today.

An older person gives up on an aged care application halfway through, not because they don't qualify, but because the process is too confusing to finish alone.

A family delays a sick child's insurance claim because gathering the paperwork feels impossible on top of everything else.

Someone walks into an asylum interview with no idea what's coming, unable to afford legal coaching beforehand.

Why it works this way

We're not trying to be the organization that helps everyone. We're trying to be the small piece that lets the organizations already doing that do it better.

01 — We build

Built with whoever's closest to the problem

Sometimes that's a staff member who sees the same bottleneck every week. Just as often, it's someone actually going through it — the person best placed to know exactly where the process breaks down. Either way, we build one specific AI tool with them, so the people coming through that organization's door can get help faster and with less friction.

02 — We verify

Checked by someone who knows the field

Before a tool is used with anyone else, a professional connected to that field — often a lawyer the organization already works with — reviews it for accuracy. We never present a tool as legal advice, and neither should they.

03 — We leave

Ownership goes to the room, not to us

The finished tool doesn't belong to Lucid, and it doesn't belong to one organization either. It's shared with every organization doing similar work, staff are trained to run it, and they train the next person. We're not in the room anymore.

The process

Eight steps, then we're gone

This is the same sequence for every organization we work with, regardless of sector — it's what makes the model repeatable instead of bespoke.

01

Connect

We partner with an organization supporting people who need help — a domestic violence service, a legal aid clinic, and similar.

02

Cover the basics

The organization confirms insurance for volunteer work, and we agree the scope in writing — including that Lucid never provides legal advice.

03

Build together, using AI

We work directly with a client or staff member to design a prompt-based tool built around whatever specific need that client has — document organizing, triage, court prep, and more.

04

Verify

A relevant professional — commonly a lawyer the organization already has access to — reviews the tool for accuracy before anyone else uses it.

05

Share, don't restrict

The tool is shared freely with the organization we built it with, and with any other organization doing similar work. No one owns it exclusively.

06

Train

We train staff on how to run the tool and how to teach it to someone else — the training is the handover.

07

Multiply

From here on, staff train any new person who needs it. No fee, and no need to involve us again.

08

Step back

Our role ends. The organization runs the tool independently, and can pass it on indefinitely.

What gets built

A few examples, by sector

Every toolkit follows the same shape — an organizer, a tracker, or a walkthrough — adapted to what a specific organization's clients actually face. Tap a sector to see examples.

Domestic Violence Advocacy

+
Organizer
Document Organizer

Logs and categorizes evidence — messages, photos, incident dates — into a structured file ready to hand to a lawyer or police.

Walkthrough
Court Prep Walkthrough

Prepares someone for a protection order hearing: what to expect, likely questions, what to bring.

Builder
Safety Plan Builder

Guides someone through building a personal safety plan — contacts, safe locations, what to pack — in their own words.

Immigration & Refugee Support

+
Tracker
Application Document Tracker

Checks required documents against a visa or asylum application, flagging what's missing.

Builder
Timeline Builder

Helps someone reconstruct an accurate personal history for an asylum claim or interview.

Walkthrough
Interview Prep Walkthrough

Plain-language prep for an immigration interview and how to organize supporting answers.

Disability Advocacy

+
Assistant
Benefits Application Assistant

Breaks a complex disability benefits application into steps, prompting for the right documentation.

Organizer
Medical Evidence Organizer

Compiles medical records and specialist letters needed to support a claim or appeal.

Builder
Appeal Letter Builder

Structures a benefits appeal in the person's own words, prompting for the evidence that supports it.

Elder Protective Services

+
Log
Financial Abuse Evidence Log

Organizes bank statements and suspicious transactions into a clear, chronological record.

Tracker
Guardianship Document Tracker

Keeps the paperwork trail for a guardianship or power-of-attorney matter organized.

Walkthrough
Report Preparation Walkthrough

Guides someone through what to document before reporting suspected abuse or exploitation.

Foster Care Support

+
Organizer
Caregiver Document Organizer

Builder

Walkthrough
